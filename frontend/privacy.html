Privacy Policy

How we handle information provided to us.

Splendid Studio respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, place an order or contact us.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, account information, order details and information you provide when communicating with us.

2. Uploaded Files

When you attach files to an order, those files may be stored so that Splendid Studio can review and work on your request. Please only upload materials that you have permission to provide.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to manage accounts, process orders, communicate with customers, provide requested services and improve the Splendid Studio experience.

4. Order Information

Information connected to an order may be accessed by authorized Splendid Studio personnel when necessary to process, manage or complete that order.

5. Communication

We may use the contact information you provide to communicate with you about your account, orders, requests or support enquiries.

6. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

Some parts of the service may rely on third-party technologies or communication services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Your Information

If you have questions about information associated with your account or order, contact Splendid Studio so we can discuss your request.

9. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to our services or how information is handled.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Splendid Studio directly.
